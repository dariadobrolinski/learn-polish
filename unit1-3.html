Digraphs & Trigraph

Unit 1, Section 3


A diagraph is a combination of two letters representing only one sound. A trigraph is the same thing but a combination of three letters. Don't be frighetned, most of these sounds exist in English!


CZ - Like 'ch' in 'chat.'

  • Example: Czekam (cheh-kah-m) meaning "I'm waiting".


SZ - like 'sh' in 'show'.

  • Example: Szal (sh-ah-l) meaning "shawl" or "scarf".


RZ - Exactly like Ż.

  • Example: Rzecz (rz-eh-ch) meaning "thing".


DZ - Pronounced like in 'podzol'.

  • Example: Dzban meaning "pitcher".


DŻ - Pronounced like 'g' in gel or like 'j' in jam.

  • Example: Dżem (jeh-m) meaning jam, the fruit paste, not like traffic on the road.


DŹ - Pronounced just how it looks, D + Ź.

  • Example: Dźwięk meaning "sound".


CH - Pronounced just like H is in English.

  • Example: Choroba (ho-ro-bah) meaning "illness".


DZI - This is the one Polish trigraph, pronounced like "DŹ"

  • Example Dziękuję meaning "thank you".
