The Letters C, W, & J

Unit 1, Section 1


The Letter C:

Unless C is followed by the letter 'i', it is pronounced 'ts', as in 'tsunami', never 'k' like in the word 'car'.

  • Example: The word 'co' (what) is pronounced 'tso'.


The Letter W:

W is always pronounced 'v' as in 'vulgar'. Never pronounced 'w' like in the word 'whale'.

  • Example: The word 'Wietnam' (Vietnam) is pronounced how Vietnam is in English.


The Letter J:

J is pronounced like 'y' like as in 'young'.

  • Example: The word 'lojalny' (loyal), is pronounced 'lo-yal-neh'.
